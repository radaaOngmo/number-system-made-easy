Octal to decimal

Use powers of eight to convert octal numbers to decimal.

LEARN THE CONCEPT

Key idea

Multiply each octal digit by its positional power of eight, starting with 8⁰ at the right, then add the results.

WORKED EXAMPLE

25₈ = 2×8 + 5 = 21₁₀.

Quick check

Convert 10₈ to decimal.

Answer: 8
