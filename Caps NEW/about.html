THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE PROJECT

Learning is better together.

Meet the five-member team behind Number System Made Easy, a digital learning resource designed to help Grade 9 DTI students understand number systems with confidence.

OUR STORY

Making number systems feel simpler.

Number System Made Easy was developed as a capstone digital artefact to make number-system concepts more approachable for Grade 9 learners. The portal brings explanations, worked examples, practice opportunities and an assessment together in one place.

We believe that students learn more confidently when difficult ideas are broken into manageable steps and they have a chance to practise at their own pace.
