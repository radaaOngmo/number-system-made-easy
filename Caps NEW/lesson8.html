Binary to hexadecimal

Group binary digits in sets of four to convert to hexadecimal.

LEARN THE CONCEPT

Key idea

Group binary digits into sets of four from the right. Add leading zeros if necessary, then convert each group to one hexadecimal digit.

WORKED EXAMPLE

11010110₂ → 1101 0110 → D6₁₆.

Quick check

How many bits are in each group for hexadecimal?

Answer: Four
