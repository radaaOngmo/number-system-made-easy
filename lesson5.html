Binary to octal

Convert binary to octal by grouping bits in threes.

LEARN THE CONCEPT

Key idea

Group binary digits into sets of three starting from the right. Add leading zeros to the left if needed, then replace each group with its octal digit.

WORKED EXAMPLE

110101₂ → 110 101 → 6 5 → 65₈.

Quick check

Convert 111₂ to octal.

Answer: 7
