LESSON 07 OF 10

Decimal to binary

Convert decimal integers to binary using repeated division by two.

LEARN THE CONCEPT

Key idea

Divide the decimal integer repeatedly by two and record each remainder. Read the remainders from bottom to top to get the binary number.

WORKED EXAMPLE

13 ÷ 2 = 6 r1; 6 ÷ 2 = 3 r0; 3 ÷ 2 = 1 r1; 1 ÷ 2 = 0 r1. Answer: 1101₂.

Quick check

Convert decimal 10 to binary.

Answer: 1010
