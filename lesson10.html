Decimal and hexadecimal

Practise converting numbers between decimal and hexadecimal.

LEARN THE CONCEPT

Key idea

To convert decimal to hexadecimal, divide by 16 repeatedly and read remainders upward. For hexadecimal to decimal, multiply each digit by its power of 16 and add.

WORKED EXAMPLE

45₁₀ = 2×16 + 13 = 2D₁₆, because D represents 13.

Quick check

What is decimal 16 in hexadecimal?

Answer: 10
